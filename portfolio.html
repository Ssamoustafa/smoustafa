About

Quality is a team capability.

I approach testing as an engineering discipline: start with risk, build reusable quality capabilities, and create evidence that helps teams make better release decisions.

I work at the intersection of software quality, delivery, and engineering. My role goes beyond building automated checks: I define strategy and planning, handle QA refinement, own specialist capabilities across an ART, and help teams choose the right evidence for accessibility, visual quality, performance, UI, and API risk. I’m especially interested in using AI to increase leverage without replacing deterministic checks or human judgment.

01

Start with risk

Choose coverage based on user impact and release risk, not test volume alone.

02

Build shared capabilities

Create approaches and feedback loops that teams can use repeatedly—not isolated test scripts.

03

Keep judgment in the loop

Automation and AI provide evidence; people decide what works for users and what is ready to release.

Approach

A clear route to confidence.

Quality works best when it is considered throughout delivery, from understanding risk to learning from every test run.

01 · UNDERSTAND

Map the risk

Clarify the user journey, system behavior, and the failures that would matter most.

02 · DESIGN

Choose the right test

Use the most useful level and technique instead of automating everything by default.

03 · ENABLE

Build shared capability

Create reusable approaches, automation, and guidance that teams can apply as the product evolves.

04 · DECIDE

Turn evidence into action

Make uncertainty visible, shorten diagnosis, and help teams make informed release decisions.
